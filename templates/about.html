🌿 AI-Powered Health & Wellness

NutriTrack

Eat Smart. Track Better. Live Healthy.

NutriTrack is an intelligent food recognition and nutrition tracking web platform designed to simplify healthy living through computer vision AI, tailored macro targets, and ingredient-based meal suggestions.

🚀 Core NutriTrack Capabilities

👤

Personalized Targets

Calculates BMR & TDEE using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula to determine personalized daily caloric and macronutrient targets.

📸

Smart Meal Upload

Upload food photos via drag-and-drop, camera snap, or manual ingredient list entry with meal category tagging.

🤖

Computer Vision AI

Automated computer vision detects multiple food items on the plate and estimates portion size and confidence.

🥗

Nutrition Database

77+ Indian and continental dishes with exact macros (calories, protein, carbs, fats) per standard serving unit.

✏️

User Reviews & Edits

Transparent confirmation interface allowing users to adjust portion sizes, add missed items, or swap ingredients.

📊

Daily Analytics & Ideas

Interactive charts, remaining calorie alerts, and smart ingredient-based recipe suggestions for dinner or next meals.
